# Timio v0.7.0 — Retention & Reactivation Engine

## Goal

Turn CRM history into an actionable owner workflow: identify clients who have exceeded their normal return interval and surface them for reactivation.

## Backend

- `GET /me/businesses/:id/retention/reactivation?segment=all|due|at_risk|lost`
- deterministic scoring based on completed visit history, return interval, lateness, visits and spend;
- future booked/confirmed appointments are excluded;
- tenant-scoped queries;
- summary counts for due / at risk / lost;
- first 100 candidates returned in score order.

## Frontend

- Today dashboard shows a Retention card when candidates exist;
- campaign tab gets a Reactivation Engine panel;
- quick segment filters;
- client rows open existing CRM card;
- no real message is sent by the engine itself.

## Algorithm

For clients with at least one completed visit:

1. calculate days since last completed visit;
2. if there are multiple visits, calculate average interval between completed visits;
3. expected interval is clamped to 14–120 days, with 30 days as the first-visit baseline;
4. classify into due / at_risk / lost using progressively wider thresholds;
5. exclude clients with a future booked/confirmed appointment;
6. score candidates from lateness + visit frequency + lifetime spend.

This is intentionally transparent MVP logic. It is a foundation for future retention experiments and AI recommendations, not an opaque AI decision.

## Important limitation

Campaign sending still uses the existing Telegram-only implementation. Reactivation identifies *who* should be contacted; channel orchestration and consent-aware campaigns remain the next step.
