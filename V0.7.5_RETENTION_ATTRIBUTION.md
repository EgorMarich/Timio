# Timio v0.7.5 — Retention Attribution

## Goal
Close the loop from retention message to booking, completed visit and revenue.

## Attribution rule
An appointment is attributed to a campaign recipient when `appointments.createdAt` is after the recipient message `sentAt` and no more than 30 days later. This avoids counting appointments that were already booked before the campaign.

## Persisted attribution
`campaign_recipients` stores `bookedAppointmentId` and `completedAppointmentId` together with existing timestamps. Performance reads are idempotent and backfill these fields when the campaign is inspected.

## Metrics
- recipients
- delivered
- failed
- booked
- completed
- booking conversion
- completion conversion
- attributed revenue

The MVP remains deterministic. No AI inference is used for attribution.
