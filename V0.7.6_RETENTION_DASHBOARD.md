# Timio v0.7.6 — Retention Dashboard

## Goal
Make the deterministic retention loop visible as a business metric, not only as an action inside Campaigns.

## Backend
Added `GET /me/businesses/:id/retention/dashboard?days=7|30|90`.

The endpoint is tenant-scoped and returns:
- retention/reactivation campaigns for the selected period;
- recipients, delivered and failed messages;
- attributed bookings and completed visits;
- attributed revenue;
- booking/completion conversion;
- current reactivation summary (`total`, `due`, `atRisk`, `lost`);
- daily campaign performance for the period.

Attribution remains deterministic and uses the v0.7.5 rule: an appointment must be created after the message was sent and within 30 days.

## Frontend
Campaigns/Marketing now starts with a Retention Dashboard:
- 7 / 30 / 90 day period switch;
- clients to return now;
- delivered contacts;
- bookings;
- attributed revenue;
- funnel from recipients → delivered → booked → completed;
- last seven sending days;
- explicit note that attributed revenue is not ROI because channel costs are not yet modeled;
- direct action to open the reactivation campaign flow.

The dashboard refreshes after a new campaign is sent.

## Product consequence
The retention loop is now measurable:

`identify → contact → book → visit → revenue`

This creates the data foundation for v0.8 AI Retention Intelligence.
