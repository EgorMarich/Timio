# Timio v0.8.5 — Onboarding & Activation

## Goal

Turn a newly registered account into an activated business with a measurable first-value path.

## Activation definition

A business is **Activated** when all six conditions are true:

1. Business profile exists with name and timezone.
2. At least 3 services exist.
3. At least 1 staff member exists.
4. At least 1 working day is configured.
5. At least 1 client exists.
6. At least 1 appointment exists.

Activation is derived server-side from real business data; analytics does not trust only frontend events.

## Onboarding flow

`Business → Services → Staff → Schedule → Client → First appointment → Activated`

The UI requires three services and one staff member so a fresh workspace is immediately useful. Existing businesses are not forced through this wizard.

## Activation telemetry

`onboarding_events` records important milestones:

- business_created
- service_created
- staff_created
- schedule_configured
- client_created
- appointment_created
- activation_completed

The API also exposes current activation state and counts through:

`GET /me/businesses/:id/onboarding`

and accepts explicit product events through:

`POST /me/businesses/:id/onboarding/events`

The activation state remains deterministic and can be reconstructed even if a frontend event was missed.

## Product metric

The primary activation metric is:

`Activated Businesses / Businesses that started onboarding`

Next releases can use onboarding events to build step conversion and time-to-value reporting.
