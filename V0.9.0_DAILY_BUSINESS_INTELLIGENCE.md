# Timio v0.9.0 — Daily Business Intelligence

## Goal
Turn the Today dashboard into a deterministic daily operating brief for the business owner.

## API
`GET /me/businesses/:id/daily-intelligence`

Returns business-local date/timezone, today's appointment/revenue state, capacity utilization, prioritized actions, next appointments and retention signals.

## Principles
- Business timezone is the source of truth for the day boundary.
- AI is not required for correctness; this release is deterministic.
- The engine recommends actions but never books, sends or changes appointment state automatically.
- Existing retention intelligence remains the source for reactivation candidates.
- Revenue uses service prices currently stored in Timio, consistent with the existing analytics implementation.

## Daily actions
1. Unconfirmed appointments.
2. Past active visits that need a result (completed/no-show).
3. Clients requiring reactivation.
4. Significant free capacity.
5. Healthy/no critical action state.

## Capacity
Working minutes are calculated from staff/business working hours. Occupied intervals are merged per staff member, producing booked minutes, free minutes, utilization and free blocks.

## Frontend
The Today tab now contains a Daily Business Intelligence card with KPIs, actions and the next five visits. Actions route to the existing Timio modules; no duplicate business logic is introduced.

## Known limitations
- Historical service price snapshots are still not stored; revenue follows the existing analytics behavior.
- Capacity is aggregate and intentionally not service-specific; service-aware empty-slot intelligence is the next release (`v0.9.1`).
- Date aggregation is business-local, but appointment timestamps remain UTC instants in PostgreSQL.
